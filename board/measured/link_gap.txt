# Frame-gap statistics — new firmware

`link clr` clears gap_last/min/max/sum. `stall` is the live "ms since the last
completed frame"; it is not cleared by `link clr`. gap_last and gap_max update
when a frame completes, so a long silence appears in gap_max when the next
frame arrives.

| phase | stall | gap_last | gap_min | gap_max | gap_sum | gap_valid | live |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| clear + read | 22927 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| streaming | 0 | 28 | 16 | 49 | 4858 | 1 | 1 |
| after stop | 5645 | 35 | 16 | 49 | 9999 | 1 | 0 |
| after restart | 2845 | 34 | 16 | 22061 | 32999 | 1 | 0 |

The restart row is the bad-interval case: `gap_max=22061 ms` is the long
silence between the last frame of the previous stream and the first frame of
the restart, recorded when that frame completed. The 8-bit/16-bit counters
saturate instead of wrapping; the maximum never rolls over.
