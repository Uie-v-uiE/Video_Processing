Same-Frame Pixel-Level A/B Display
the picture itself becomes the measurement instrument

A Zynq-7020 fabric receives 512×300 RGB565 video over GbE, SD card or its own generated test pattern, applies geometry and effects entirely in the PL, and drives 1024×600 HDMI in which every column left of an adjustable seam is the unprocessed frame and every column right of it is the processed one — one read address, one clock cycle, one scanline. No capture card, no screen recording, no second panel: misalignment, dropped columns and out-of-range fill become visible on the panel.

01  What is new

One frame, two imagesdisplay = instrument

The seam mixes original and processed pixels per column, per clock; its position is a runtime parameter (0–100 %) and can be pinned in image space so it survives zoom and rotation. That turns a demo trick into a measuring device: geometry errors appear as a step, a gap or a black wedge across the seam.

Kept criterion: after split <n> the seam marker must not move — a parameter change without a datapath change fails.

One coordinate operator for zoom, rotation and interpolationfixed point

zfit solves the fit factor in 3 DSP48 blocks, zmap maps every pixel through 8 DSP48 blocks, rotation runs inverse-mapped with two linear interpolations. Eight ratios from 0.25× to 2.00× plus auto-breath and auto-fit, so a rotated frame always stays inside the panel — checked by a two-sided bench test, not by an eye.

Ratio, source and fit state read back over AXI GPIO: the on-screen cell and the register value are the same number.

A datapath whose latency is a constant15 cycles, always

Six pipeline stages, nine independently switchable algorithms, each window stage keeping its own column tag — so total latency is 15 cycles and content offset −4 rows for every combination. The fabric exposes one number, u_pipe.LATENCY; the read scheduler never re-derives it, and any drift fails a bench test.

That invariant is what keeps the A/B seam honest while effects are switched live.

03  Verification & reproducibility

Three layers that do not substitute for each other: 82 unit testbenches; a full-screen bench walking the whole design into 141 per-pixel criteria with RTL and bench md5 in the report header; and a board layer only a person can judge — 36 eye-check rows, each giving command, expected image and what a "no" would mean.

Every criterion must be able to turn red: passing on an empty set counts as failure, and each fix carries a mutation control that reddens exactly the criterion it claims. One command rebuilds every report; one command exports the submission and refuses to write it if a dead link, a stale name or an absolute path remains.

Skill pack  49 practice entries decoupled from this topic — scope, usage, failure conditions, verified reuse, including the negative results.

04  Control plane & open scope

A few hundred lines of bare-metal C give a serial command surface (source select, seam, zoom, angle, threshold, gamma table, counter clear) and three register windows that read back the state the panel shows. The PS never touches the video path: ingress, reassembly, ARP, ICMP and the health counters are all fabric, so the board answers ping while streaming, and hot-unplugging the cable or the card switches source and switches back without a reset.

Open by design, with numbers attached: five ingress endpoints have no window after that constraint was withdrawn; the four TMDS output groups are declared BARE after measurement; the rotation angle has no serial read-back. These are listed rather than resolved by loosening a check.

Three things this project actually learned. ① A number without its condition is not a result — every metric row names the file it came from, and absolute WNS deltas are deliberately not counted as gain. ② The check needs a test too: a criterion that cannot turn red, or that passes on an empty set, is treated as a failure of the tooling, not of the design. ③ Negative results are cheap to keep and expensive to lose — six timing cuts are published as rejected with their readings, which is what makes the accepted ones credible.
Every number above comes from a report shipped with the submission — build/reports/gates.txt (24 items, identity line), build/timing_summary.rpt, build/utilization.rpt, data/metrics.csv (each row naming its own evidence file) and the cold-boot serial capture under board/. No frame-grab instrument was used, so no claim on this poster depends on one; the frame-interval row is explicitly a PL-internal measurement. Tool-estimated power/temperature and on-die XADC readings are printed as two different quantities.
